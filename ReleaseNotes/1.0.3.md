# Pinnest 1.0.3

This release adds more ways to create and manage image references without interrupting your workspace.

## New image intake

- Choose one or more images with **Pin from File…** in the menu bar.
- Open image files directly with Pinnest from Finder's **Open With** menu.
- Use **Pin with Pinnest** from the macOS Share menu for image content from supported apps.
- Drag image files or image data directly onto the Pinnest menu-bar icon.
- All external images open at the center of the active display.

## Hidden pins

- Use the yellow traffic-light button to collapse a pin into a compact floating indicator.
- Drag the indicator without restoring the image.
- Hover to preview the hidden image; click to restore it to its previous geometry and settings.
- Hidden indicators remain interactive even when Piercing was enabled.

## Pin management

- Close pins on the current Desktop, other Desktops, or every Desktop.
- Hold Option to preserve hidden pins while closing visible pins in those scopes.
- Use **Close All Hidden Pins** to remove hidden indicators across all Desktops.

## Fixes

- Prevent transformed image corners from protruding outside the hidden indicator during collapse.
- Prevent Open With launches from opening the About window.
