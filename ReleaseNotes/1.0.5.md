# Pinnest 1.0.5

Fixes **Check for Updates** in the sandboxed app. Sparkle now downloads and installs signed updates through its dedicated services without granting network access to Pinnest itself.

**Upgrading from 1.0.4 or earlier:** Quit Pinnest, download `Pinnest-1.0.5.zip` from this release, unzip it, and replace Pinnest in Applications. Open the new copy. This one-time manual installation is necessary because the older updater cannot fetch the update feed.
